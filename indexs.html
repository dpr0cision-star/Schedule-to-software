Schedule to Takeoff

Drop in a door schedule as a PDF or photo. The page reads each opening right in your browser, with no account and no per-use charge. You check the rows, and it saves a .txt import file in the e-mullions Openings column order.

1 Add the door schedule

Drop PDFs, photos or copied text here, or click to choose. You can also paste a screenshot or copied schedule lines anywhere on the page.

Tick only the sheets that hold the schedule. PDFs exported from the drawing software read best, because the text is already in the file. Photos and scans are read by OCR, which is slower and makes more mistakes: turn a sideways thumbnail upright with Rotate, and use a straight, close, sharp shot of just the schedule.

2 Job defaults

These go on every opening, the same way you would fill them down in e-mullions. Leave a box empty to use what the schedule says (or nothing). They are remembered on this device.
